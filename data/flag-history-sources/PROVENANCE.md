# What this folder is, and why nothing here is used yet

Carried over from the Flag-History repository when its 245 pages were merged
into this atlas (Phase 1, 27 September 2026). Kept so the provenance of that
work is not lost when the old repository is archived.

**Nothing in this folder is read by the build, and nothing here is published.**
It sits in `data/` rather than `site/public/` deliberately.

## The two CSVs, and the open question on them

`countries.csv` (108 KB) and `states.csv` (652 KB) are not flag research. Their
columns — `iso2`, `wikiDataId`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `timezone` — identify
them as the widely mirrored *countries-states-cities* dataset rather than
anything authored here.

**Their licence is not documented anywhere in the repository they came from.**
The README carried over is a single line. That dataset is variously distributed
under ODbL and under CC BY-SA depending on the mirror, and those two impose
different obligations — ODbL's share-alike would reach anything derived from it.

So: **not used, not published, not attributed**, until somebody establishes
where the copy came from and under what terms. This atlas already ships seven
licences and fails its own build when a source is used without declaring one;
an eighth arriving by inheritance does not get a pass because it arrived
quietly.

`states.csv` is genuinely tempting — coordinates for sub-national divisions
worldwide would help place records. That is exactly why it is written down here
rather than used on the quiet.
