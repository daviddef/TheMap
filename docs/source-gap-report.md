## Notes and limits

- **Demand is thin evidence.** Family research marks come from the published research-sessions file (the sessions' own work); a country with no marks may simply not have been researched yet.
- **"Not catalogued" compares websites.** A provider row whose site is on a different host from Wikidata's (a regional portal, a catalogue sub-domain) shows its institution as not catalogued. Expect some false gaps.
- **Wikidata is uneven.** It has many small municipal offices and few archives in some countries. A small reservoir is not proof of a small supply.
- **Nothing here was opened.** The Wikidata and world-archives rows are unverified; see the notes in those files.
- **Sources with country `*`** (worldwide) are excluded from every count; a source listing more than three countries is not counted as research in any of them.
- **A source with several countries counts once in each**, so the per-country columns add up to more than the catalogue total.
