# Head names still unplaced: for your decision, 11 Nov 2026

75 names, 13,567 books. These are the names of 100 or more books that the researchers could not settle on evidence, or whose only point was refused by the gate. Nothing here is a finding. A name is placed only when you (or a page we can cite) settle it; the usual reason is that the name belongs to more than one place and the collections it sits under cover both.

Each line: name (books) · what the researcher found. Where a researcher suggested a point it is named in the sentence, so a one-word answer from you ("use it", "leave") is enough.
