# Why the matcher refuses so much, and why loosening it would be wrong

Measured 21 September 2026, against 985,965 unplaced volumes. Written
because every one of these refusals looks, from the outside, like a bug
worth fixing — and fixing them the obvious way would put wrong dots on
the map.

## What is actually left unplaced

| shape of the name | names | volumes | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| a plain, ordinary place name | 16,960 | 738,353 | 74.9% |
| has a comma | 7,921 | 104,124 | 10.6% |
| a saint's dedication | 817 | 52,574 | 5.3% |
| has a bracket | 3,476 | 33,151 | 3.4% |
| a number in it | 833 | 21,445 | 2.2% |
| one word, under five letters | 605 | 20,369 | 2.1% |
| non-Latin script | 3,386 | 12,014 | 1.2% |
| five words or more | 225 | 3,935 | 0.4% |

Three quarters of what is missing is not malformed, not a filing label
and not a puzzle. It is ordinary town names.

## The reason is gazetteer coverage, and nothing cleverer

Of 21,176 plain names, **20,253 are not in the gazetteer at all** —
745,590 volumes. The gazetteer holds 45,985 places for the whole world.
The world has rather more.

So the lever is the demand-driven exonym harvest, which asks Wikidata
only for names this atlas has actually failed on. Not better matching
against a gazetteer that does not contain the answer.

## The trap: 628 names the gazetteer DOES hold, in another country

This is the one that looks like free money. 628 plain unplaced names
exist in the gazetteer under a different country from the collection
that filed them. Sixty-six of those are inside the collection's
historical filing group, which is the test for a plausible crossing.

Resolve them and most would be wrong. The biggest, with what they
actually are:

| name | filed under | gazetteer says | what the book almost certainly means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sala | IT | SK (Šaľa) | Sala Consilina or Sala Baganza, in Italy |
| Albi | IT | FR (Albi) | Albi, a comune in Calabria |
| Celle | IT | DE (Celle) | Celle Ligure or Celle di Bulgheria |
| Galati | IT | RO (Galați) | Galati Mamertino, in Sicily |
| Pace | IT | FR | Pace del Mela, in Sicily |

They are false friends: Italian places missing from a thin gazetteer,
which happen to share a name with a town that is in it. The filing group
says the crossing is *possible*, which is not the same as likely.

The two that are real crossings are the Irish pairs — **Sligo** filed
under GB and **Coleraine** filed under IE — where the border genuinely
runs through the filing.

## So the rule stays

A crossing needs two distinct agreeing levels, one of them an exonym,
and now also that the place lie within 1,500 km of the filing country.
That is strict enough to refuse Sala and Albi, and loose enough to have
found 195 Friulian villages under Austrian Küstenland collections and
the whole Slovene Karst under Italian ones.

**If a future session is tempted to place those 628 because the numbers
look easy: this is what they are.** The right way to reach them is to
put the missing Italian, Slovak and Romanian towns into the gazetteer,
so the home country answers first and the question never arises.
