# Which national surname registers can be added? — 16 October 2026

The audit found the surname data lopsided: thirteen registers are held, and the
Netherlands, Sweden, Germany, Austria, Czechia, Portugal, the UK and Hungary are
not, so a name's «countries» reflects which registers we hold and not where it
occurs. This is what a search found on each, today. **Nothing here has been
downloaded, and no source is assumed to exist.** Each line says what the search
returned and what is still unverified.

| country | what exists | bulk, open? | next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Netherlands | The Familienamenbank (Nederlandse Familienamenbank) covers about 300,000 surnames of Dutch nationals in 2007 and about 120,000 from the 1947 census. It is run by the Centraal Bureau voor Genealogie, with the Meertens Instituut as the former host. | **No download or licence found.** A 2010 repository record lists zero files; a Meertens text says personal data in the name databases were restricted after a 2009 privacy review. | The letter is drafted (`requests/meertens-familienamenbank.md`) and waiting for David. It should go to the CBG, which now runs it. |
| Sweden | Statistics Sweden (SCB) counts surnames; its page says about 759,000 surnames exist, each spelling counted separately. Skatteverket runs a «how many have this name» search. | **Not confirmed.** Older SCB documents (2002-03) say surnames with fewer than five bearers are not released. A CC BY 4.0 dataset of names by country of birth exists via a data archive, but it is a third-party redistribution and not a surname list. | Ask SCB whether a full surname-and-bearer-count file exists and on what licence. |
| Czechia | Counts come from the Ministry of the Interior's population register; the statistics office says it has no access to surnames. A 2006 frequency list was published. | **No.** The ministry's own statistics pages carry a caveat that it is not authorised to publish statistics of this kind. | The Národní archiv letter (sent) is about places, not names. A separate question to the Ministry of the Interior would be needed; low expectation. |
| Germany | No national register of surname counts was found. | No. | None; do not substitute a commercial or crowd-sourced list as if it were a register. |
| Austria, Portugal, UK, Hungary | Not searched in this pass. | Unknown. | Search each national statistics office before assuming anything. |

**What this means for the site.** Until a register is added, the surname pages
should keep saying which registers were consulted and which were not, so that a
country with no register is never read as a country without the name. That change
is one of the audit's recommendations and is not made here.
