# 85 · Region eras that sources contradict (found 18 Oct 2026)

Ten readers assessed every era of 25 older regions (`approx`, `contested`) against Wikipedia and an independent source, and recorded where a source contradicts the era's dates or label. **Nothing here has been applied to `data/regions.json`**: each is a proposed correction for a decision, because the label or date moves what the family sites say beside an event. Assessments themselves are in `data/region-provenance.json` and published in `context.json`.


## bosnia

- **1463** — Polity: 'Eyalet, then Vilayet' only from 1580 / 1865-67; 1463-1580 sanjak of Bosnia.
- **1941** — Regime tag 'german' for the NDH is inconsistent with the Vojna krajina region, which tags the same NDH 'croatia'.

## carnia

- **1797** — Austria 1798-1805, extend to 1806 or start the Italian era 1805.
- **1866** — Split out 1943-1945: OZAK / Cossack Land / partisan Free Zone of Carnia (1944).

## carniola

- **1500** — Start 1500 is a floor, not an event; polity 'Inner Austria' only from 1564 (before that Habsburg hereditary lands / Austrian Circle 1512).
- **1813** — Crown land status: Kingdom of Illyria 1816-1849 then crown land from 1849 (Hrvatska enciklopedija); the era label 'Duchy of Carniola and Lower Styria' hides that.
- **1918** — Note: the ring includes Trieste/Gorizia, so 1918-1941 is partly Kingdom of Italy, as the note already says.
- **1941** — Add the Independent State of Croatia to the list of occupiers; end is May 1945, and Slovenian Littoral/Trieste are in the next era's dispute.
- **1945** — Trieste and Gorizia area did not pass to Socialist Slovenia on the same terms; consider a note that 1945-1954 Trieste is not Yugoslav.

## dalmatia

- **1200** — Start 1200 has no event behind it; Hrvatska enciklopedija offers 1102 (Coloman) or 1358 (Peace of Zadar). Suggest from 1102 or label as floor.
- **1420** — Polity: add that the hinterland (e.g. Imotski) was Ottoman until 1717 and the Acquisto Nuovo (Sinj, Knin etc.) only after 1699/1718 wars.
- **1805** — Era 1805-1813 omits the 1813-1815 interregnum; and 1809-1813 Dalmatia was formally part of the Illyrian Provinces (Hrvatska enciklopedija: from 1809-1811), not 'French Illyria' from 1805.
- **1918** — Zadar enclave and Lastovo were Italian 1920-1941, not Kingdom of Yugoslavia, as the data already hints at only in 1941.
- **1943** — Polity 'German occupation' is incomplete: the territory was nominally NDH after 10 Sept 1943, with German military control, Partisan-held islands/areas, and Zadar nominally Italian.
- **1991** — Polity 'Croatia - Split-Dalmatia' hides 1991-95 northern Dalmatia hinterland (Knin) under the unrecognised RSK.

## east-prussia

- **1226** — Era start should read c.1230 (conquest) with 1226 as the grant.
- **1871** — polity: 'German Empire - Province of Prussia (to 1878), then Province of East Prussia'; Memel Territory not German 1920-1939.
- **1945** — Second sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaliningrad_Oblast ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oder%E2%80%93Neisse_line

## hungary

- **1526** — 1526 is the start of a war, not of Ottoman rule (1541); 1526-1541 was a rival-kings contest.
- **1699** — Second source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Hungary. Banat of Temesvar is Ottoman until 1718.
- **1918** — Era start 1918 is the collapse; legal change is 1920/1921. Sopron plebiscite 1921 exception.
- **1945** — polity 'People's Republic of Hungary' is 1949-1989; 1945-1949 was the Soviet-supervised Provisional government, then Second Republic (1946).

## imotski

- **1493** — Polity 'Sanjak of Klis' is unverified: no fetched source places Imotski in it (a kadi resided in Topana fortress). Start 1492 vs 1493.
- **1918** — Split the era: 1918-1941 Yugoslavia, 1941-1945 NDH (as Imotski is listed in NDH), 1945-1991 SFRY.

## istria-central

- **1805** — Habsburg 1374-1809; Napoleonic Illyrian Provinces 1809-1813 (Pazin stayed Austrian through 1805-1809; the Kingdom of Italy era does not apply to Pazin).
- **1815** — Austrian Empire (Kingdom of Illyria 1816-49, Küstenland from 1849, Austria-Hungary from 1867); start 1813/14.
- **1918** — Italian occupation from Nov 1918; Italian sovereignty de jure from Rapallo (12 Nov 1920).

## istria-south

- **1331** — Venice: Rovinj 1283, Pula 1331.
- **1805** — Napoleonic Kingdom of Italy 1806-1809/10 (Pressburg 1805), then Illyrian Provinces 1809/10-1813.
- **1813** — Austrian Empire; Illyria 1816-49; Küstenland from 1849.
- **1945** — Split: Allied Military Government (Pula enclave) 1945-15 Sept 1947; Yugoslavia elsewhere from 1945.

## istria-west

- **1331** — Venice from 1267 (Poreč), gradually to 1358 (Buje). Also note: Poreč diocese was Parenzo not 'Pola' (polity text on the later eras says 'diocese of Pola'; unverified, flag).
- **1805** — Napoleonic Kingdom of Italy 1806-1809/10 (Pressburg 1805), then Illyrian Provinces 1809/10-1813.
- **1813** — Austrian Empire; Illyria 1816-49; Küstenland from 1849.
- **1945** — Split: Yugoslav 1945-47 (Italian claim until the treaty); Zone B (FTT) for Buje district 1947-1954; Croatia within Yugoslavia thereafter.

## kvarner

- **1200** — Rijeka under Walsee/Duino before 1466, not Frankopan.
- **1469** — Split by ground: Rijeka Habsburg 1466; Senj Hungarian-Croatian crown 1469 (Matthias Corvinus); Vinodol/Bakar Frankopan-Zrinski to 1671, then crown. Rijeka corpus separatum under Hungary 1776/1779. Military Frontier at Senj 1776.
- **1813** — Split: Austria/Illyria 1813-1822; Hungary 1822-1848; Croatia 1848-1868 (disputed); Hungarian corpus separatum 1868-1918. Senj/Vinodol under Military Frontier/Croatia, not Budapest.
- **1918** — Split: 1918-1924 disputed/Free State; Italy (Fiume) from 1924; Sušak Yugoslav.

## pomerania

- **1181** — Era start is feudal re-homage; 1121 for the duchy. Danzig was Pomerelia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gda%C5%84sk).
- **1637** — Stettin was SWEDISH until 1720: polity 'Swedish in the west, Brandenburg-Prussian in the east' hides that. Danzig/Pomerelia was Polish (Royal Prussia) 1466-1793, not Brandenburg-Prussian (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gda%C5%84sk).
- **1815** — Danzig: Prussian 1793, French Free City 1807-1814, then West Prussia from 1815; polity 'Province of Pomerania' does not cover Danzig.
- **1920** — The 1871 era should end 1918 (empire) or 1920 (Versailles in force); Danzig only from 15 Nov 1920, Jan-Nov 1920 was transitional.
- **1939** — polity: only Danzig and Pomerelia changed in 1939; Stettin/Koslin stayed German.
- **1945** — polity: Poland only for the part east of the Oder-Neisse line; Vorpommern (Stralsund, Greifswald) stayed in Germany. Second source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oder%E2%80%93Neisse_line

## posen

- **1793** — For Szubin and Bydgoszcz the Prussian era starts 1772/73 (First Partition); only Poznan itself changes in 1793. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bydgoszcz ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szubin
- **1807** — Transition 1806-1807: Polish/French control from the Nov 1806 uprising, treaty cession July 1807.
- **1815** — Second source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Warsaw
- **1918** — Szubin 1919 vs Bydgoszcz 1920: local year differs; era start approx.
- **1939** — polity: Bydgoszcz (Bromberg) was in Reichsgau Danzig-West Prussia, not Wartheland (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bydgoszcz); Szubin in Wartheland. Also https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Poland_(1939%E2%80%931945)

## santafe

- **1580** — polity: 'Spanish Empire - Viceroyalty of Peru (to 1776), then Viceroyalty of the Rio de la Plata'; era start is a lower bound (Santa Fe founded 1573)
- **1810** — polity 'Argentina - the Republic' is anachronistic before 1853: better 'United Provinces / autonomous Province of Santa Fe (sovereign from 1816)'; 1810 is the start of a transition (1810-1816), not a single year. Second source: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincia_de_Santa_Fe
- **1889** — Possible conflict: 1899 vs 1889; needs a primary source (Santa Fe law / Archivo General de la Provincia).

## vojna-krajina

- **1553** — Polity: the Habsburg Frontier did not hold Lika, Kordun or Srijem for most of this era; they were Ottoman until 1689-1699. Suggest 'Habsburg-Ottoman frontier' or note.
- **1746** — Era 1746-1848 omits the French Illyrian Provinces of 1809-1813 over the Croatian Military Frontier; regiments date from 1745-1754 depending on the part.
- **1991** — Polity 'Croatia' hides RSK in 1991-95 and UNTAES to 1998; first date should be 'de facto' for this ground.

## vojvodina

- **1699** — Start 1699 is correct for Backa and northern Srijem only; Banat is 1716-1718.
- **1848** — Era hides 1849-1860/61 crown land and Syrmia in Croatia-Slavonia; 1918 end see next era.
- **1941** — Western Srijem stayed Axis-held to April 1945, so '1944' is wrong there; formal Hungarian annexation of Backa/Baranja not verified here.
- **1944** — Regime 'serbia' for 1944-1992 is misleading; Vojvodina was Yugoslav then Serbian.
