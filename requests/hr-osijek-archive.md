**Subject:** How can a family researcher ask about your records?

Dear Sir or Madam,

I help run Record Atlas (https://recordatlas.org/), a free guide that tells family researchers where records are kept. So that we send people to you correctly, could you tell us:

1. the email address or form for research enquiries;
2. reading-room hours, and whether a visit must be booked;
3. whether you answer written requests, in which languages, and any fee;
4. where we can see which parish and civil registers you hold, and for which years.

We will publish only what you tell us about your institution, with the date. Thank you.

Kind regards,
[name]
