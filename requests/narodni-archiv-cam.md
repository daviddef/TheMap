# Draft: request to the Národní archiv for CAM's place authorities

**Not sent.** Writing to a third party on David's behalf needs his say-so. This is
ready to send from his own address, or to adapt.

**To:** na@nacr.cz (and/or digibadatelna.podpora@zao.archives.cz for the Opava portal)
**Subject:** Request for place authority records from CAM (Centrální archivní modul) for an open genealogy index

---

Dear colleagues,

I maintain **Record Atlas** (https://recordatlas.org/), a free, non-commercial,
open-source index of where genealogical records are held — which archive holds which
ground, for which years, and what it costs a researcher to look. It holds no personal
data and its own datasets are published openly.

The atlas currently draws a blank over most of the Czech Republic: of roughly 4,987
Czech places it maps, almost none can yet say which archive holds registers for them.
That is a gap in my index, not in your holdings.

The Digitální badatelna's own help page explains that archival records are linked to
authority records **shared by all Czech archives through CAM, the Central Archival
Module**, and that PLACE is one of their classes. That is precisely the thing I need:
not the records, not the scans, but the list of places and which archive's holdings
they attach to.

I would be grateful to know:

1. Whether CAM's PLACE authority records — the place name, any former or German name,
   and the archive or fonds they are linked to — can be supplied as a file, or are
   published anywhere I have not found.
2. If not as a file, whether there is an OAI-PMH, SRU or REST endpoint I may read
   politely, and at what rate you would like me to read it.
3. Whom I should credit, and under what terms.

I want to be explicit about what I have not done. The Opava portal
(digi.archives.cz) is behind a bot check, and several Czech portals return 403 to an
identified crawler. **I have not worked around any of them**, and I do not intend to.
That is why I am writing rather than collecting.

If a file is easier than an endpoint, a CSV of place name, former name, district and
holding archive would be more than enough, and I would publish the result with your
attribution and a link back to the portal.

With thanks for your time,

David Defranceski
Record Atlas — https://recordatlas.org/
