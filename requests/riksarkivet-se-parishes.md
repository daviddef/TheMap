# Draft: to Riksarkivet (Sweden) — parish coverage, asked rather than crawled

**Not sent.** Writing to a third party on David's behalf needs his say-so.

**To:** riksarkivet@riksarkivet.se
**Subject:** Request for a parish/holdings list for an open, non-commercial index of where records are held

---

Dear colleagues,

I maintain **Record Atlas** (https://recordatlas.org/), a free, non-commercial,
open-source index of where genealogical records are held — which archive holds which
ground, for which years, and at what cost to the reader. It holds no personal data.

I am writing rather than collecting, and I want to be plain about why.

**sok.riksarkivet.se/robots.txt names my crawler.** It carries
`User-agent: ClaudeBot / Disallow: /` and `User-agent: Claude-SearchBot / Disallow: /`.
I read that as the archive's clear answer and I have respected it: I have not read
your search portal, and I have not gone to riksarkivet.se or data.riksarkivet.se to
get the same material by a side door. An institution that has said no by name has
said no.

But a robots.txt governs crawlers, not correspondence — so I am asking a person
instead.

What I would like is modest and is not the records themselves:

1. A list of the **parishes (församlingar)** whose church books Riksarkivet holds,
   with the county (län) and, if it is easy, the year span held.
2. Whether such a list is already published as open data, in which case a link is all
   I need.
3. Whom to credit and under what terms.

Sweden is currently 2,363 places on this map with nothing said about where their
records are. A parish list would fix that at a stroke, and I would publish it with
your attribution and a link back.

If the answer is that you would rather I did not index your holdings at all, that is a
perfectly good answer and I will record it as such and stop asking.

Med vänliga hälsningar,

David Defranceski
Record Atlas — https://recordatlas.org/
