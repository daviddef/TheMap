Roots & Routes

Drop in your family tree file and see it drawn on the Atlas: every place tied to its country, its flag and who ruled it that year.

Your file never leaves this computer

  • It is read and drawn by code running in this browser tab. It is not uploaded, not stored on a server and not seen by anyone.
  • This page has no analytics, no cookies and no third-party scripts, and the browser is told not to let it contact any other site.
  • To place your people it downloads the Atlas's own place files for the countries your tree mentions. Our server can see which countries were asked for; it never sees your file or a name from it.
  • People who may be alive are shown on your own screen. The shareable export leaves them out, and you can hide them with one tick.

      Maybe the same person

      Same name, same sex, born within two years, not related as parent, child or spouse. Marking a pair merges nothing: it writes a link between the two records.

      Fix list

      Work down each list in the tree you keep elsewhere: copy, paste, tick. Ticks stay in this page, so download the list to keep your place.

      Offer places back to Record Atlas

      Optional, and nothing is ticked. Places this page could not read, or that you chose by hand, can help the Atlas learn them. Only the place text goes into the message: no names, no dates, no people, no counts, no file name. This page sends nothing. You tick the places you want to offer, read the message below, and send it from your own email or copy it. Places used only by living people are never listed.

      Export your GEDCOM, improved
